Research tooling for Indian equity portfolios.

Quadrion brings machine-learning scores, news-derived signals and allocation checks together so an investor can reason about an NSE-listed equity portfolio, with an explainable trail behind every output.

What it does

  • Scores NSE-listed stocks every trading day using models trained on historical market data.
  • Reads Indian financial news and turns it into sentiment signals.
  • Compares a portfolio against the investor's own target allocation, and records how each output was reached.

Where it stands

Quadrion ran as a small closed pilot earlier this year. It is now paused while it is rebuilt around a clearer evidence trail and a redesigned interface. It is not open to the public at the moment.
